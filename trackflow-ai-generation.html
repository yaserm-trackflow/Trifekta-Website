How it works

A visual sales workflow, not a random image button.

TrackFlow connects the AI concept stage with the work that comes after: quotation, approval, production, installation, and payment follow-up.

Upload the context

Add room photos, product images, project details, dimensions, materials, and placement notes.

Generate concepts

Create AI image options that match the actual client space and the item being discussed.

Collect feedback

Use generated visuals inside the client journey so the client can comment, choose, or approve faster.

Move to execution

Approved concepts become references for the team before technical drawings, production, and delivery.
